Privacy Policy – Locate Your Dentist (LYD)

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Locate Your Dentist (LYD) is a digital platform designed to connect Dental Clinics, Dental Laboratories, Dental Shops, Dental Technicians, Dental Mechanics, Dental Professionals, Job Seekers, and Patients.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Locate Your Dentist mobile application and website.

By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2.1 Information You Provide

When creating an account, we may collect:

Full Name

Email Address

Mobile Number

Business/Clinic/Lab Name

Address Information

Profile Images

Professional Information

Job-related Information

Subscription Information

2.2 Location Information

With your permission, we may collect location information to:

Show nearby dental clinics

Display nearby dental laboratories

Locate nearby dental suppliers and shops

Improve search results

Location access is optional and can be disabled through your device settings.

2.3 Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay.

We do not store:

Credit Card Numbers

Debit Card Numbers

UPI PINs

Banking Passwords

Payment providers manage all payment transactions securely.

2.4 Device Information

We may collect:

Device Type

Operating System

App Version

IP Address

Crash Reports

Diagnostic Information

We use collected information to:

Create and manage user accounts

Conduct webinars and training programs

Process subscription plans

Send notifications and updates

Improve app performance

Ensure platform security
